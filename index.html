How it works

From a finished project to a searchable archive in three steps.

01

Upload your project

Students add the title, abstract, and PDF report of their graduation project.

02

Get a suggested category

The system reads the abstract and suggests the most fitting categories, with a confidence score for each.

03

Reviewed and published

An administrator checks the category and publishes the project, so everyone can find it.
